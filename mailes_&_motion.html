Kerala

Hills & tea country

  • Munnar: rolling tea plantations, mist, and cool weather. It's the most popular hill station in the state, and Eravikulam National Park and Mattupetty Dam are close by.
  • Wayanad: forests, waterfalls, and caves like Edakkal, with a quieter feel than Munnar.
  • Vagamon: pine forests and meadows, less crowded.

Backwaters

  • Alleppey (Alappuzha): the famous houseboat stays through palm-lined canals.
  • Kumarakom: calmer backwaters, bird sanctuary, and luxury resorts.

Beaches

  • Varkala: dramatic cliffside beach with cafes along the top, good for sunsets.
  • Kovalam: a crescent-shaped beach popular for swimming and Ayurvedic spas.

Wildlife & nature

  • Thekkady (Periyar): boat safaris and spice plantations, with a chance to see elephants.
  • Athirappilly Falls: often called the "Niagara of India," best in and just after monsoon.

Culture & city

  • Fort Kochi: Chinese fishing nets, colonial streets, art cafes, and the Kochi-Muziris Biennale.
