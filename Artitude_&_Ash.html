Artitude & Ash

Scenic flight approaches

  • Maldives: seaplane transfers over turquoise atolls are often the highlight of the trip itself.
  • Queenstown, New Zealand: dramatic mountain approach, considered one of the most scenic landings in the world.
  • Lukla, Nepal: short runway on a cliffside in the Himalayas, gateway to Everest Base Camp treks.

Island & coastal escapes

  • Bora Bora / French Polynesia: overwater bungalows, only reachable by flight and boat.
  • Santorini, Greece: whitewashed cliffside towns over the Aegean Sea.
  • Seychelles: pristine beaches and granite islands.

Iconic city breaks

  • Dubai: futuristic skyline, luxury, and desert day trips.
  • Tokyo: blends ultra-modern and traditional culture.
  • Paris: classic romantic getaway.

From India specifically

  • Leh-Ladakh: flying in gives you dramatic Himalayan views, and it's much faster than the road route.
  • Andaman Islands: turquoise water and coral reefs, only practical by flight from the mainland.

Best picks for Haloway's "Flights" section:

Maldives and Queenstown both work well visually — the seaplane/turquoise water shot photographs beautifully, and Queenstown's aerial mountain approach fits the "dreamy journey" aesthetic already running through the site.
