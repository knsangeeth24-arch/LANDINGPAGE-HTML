In India

  • Nilgiri Mountain Railway (Mettupalayam to Ooty): a UNESCO-listed toy train climbing through tunnels, forests, and tea country. It's close to Coimbatore, so it's easy to do as a day trip or weekend.
  • Darjeeling Himalayan Railway: the famous "toy train" through tea gardens with Himalayan views.
  • Konkan Railway (Mumbai to Goa/Mangaluru): coastal route with rivers, tunnels, and monsoon waterfalls.
  • Kalka to Shimla: narrow-gauge line with over 100 tunnels and arched bridges.
  • Jaisalmer and Jodhpur (Rajasthan): desert forts and palaces, best by overnight train.
  • Kerala (Thiruvananthapuram to Kochi): palm-lined backwaters and coastline from the window.

Around the world

  • Glacier Express (Switzerland): slow scenic ride through the Alps.
  • Bernina Express (Switzerland to Italy): glaciers, lakes, and mountain viaducts.
  • Trans-Siberian Railway (Russia): the longest rail journey in the world, for a big adventure.
  • Japan's Shinkansen and Kyoto lines: fast, punctual, and paired with temples and cherry blossoms.
  • Flam Railway (Norway): fjords, waterfalls, and steep valleys in a short ride.

Best picks for Haloway:

The Nilgiri Mountain Railway for an Indian route with dreamy mist and greenery, and the Glacier Express for the classic "window seat" feel. Both match the soft, atmospheric look you're going for, and they'd make good clickable image cards for a "Train Journeys" page.
