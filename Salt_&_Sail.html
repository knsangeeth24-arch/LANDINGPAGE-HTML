Salt & Sail

Classic cruise destinations

  • Mediterranean (Italy, Greece, Croatia): island-hopping between Santorini, the Amalfi Coast, and Dubrovnik, with turquoise water and cliffside towns at nearly every stop.
  • Caribbean Islands: beaches, coral reefs, and colorful port towns across islands like the Bahamas, Jamaica, and St. Lucia.
  • Norwegian Fjords: dramatic cliffs, waterfalls, and quiet villages tucked into narrow inlets — especially striking on a slow-moving ship.
  • Alaska's Inside Passage: glaciers, whales, and misty coastal mountains.

Scenic river cruises

  • Nile River (Egypt): temples and ancient sites along the riverbanks.
  • Danube River (Europe): passes through Vienna, Budapest, and Bratislava.
  • Mekong River (Vietnam/Cambodia): floating markets and rural village life.

Island & archipelago ferries

  • Greek Islands ferry routes: hopping between Mykonos, Santorini, and smaller islands by ferry rather than cruise ship.
  • Greek Islands ferry routes: hopping between Mykonos, Santorini, and smaller islands by ferry rather than cruise ship.
  • Lakshadweep (India): coral atolls reached only by ship from Kochi.

Historic & unique voyages

  • Venice, Italy: arriving by water is part of the experience — canals and gondolas instead of roads.
  • Antarctica expedition cruises: for something far more remote and dramatic.
